Explore the timeline

Jain tradition understands its Dharma as eternal. This timeline distinguishes traditional accounts from periods supported by historical evidence rather than treating every sacred chronology as a modern date.

Jain cosmological tradition

Ṛṣabhanātha

Revered as the first Tirthankara of the present time-cycle. Traditional accounts place him in a vast sacred chronology, not a standard BCE date.

Tradition
Early historic period · approximate

Pārśvanātha

The 23rd Tirthankara; often discussed by scholars as an early Jain teacher with a historical setting before Mahāvīra.

Historical discussion
6th century BCE · approximate scholarly context

Mahāvīra

The 24th Tirthankara and a central teacher of the Śramaṇa traditions of ancient India. Traditional dates differ from scholarly estimates.

Historical discussion
Early centuries CE
